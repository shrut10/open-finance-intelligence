    01 / THE DATA

    Official sources.
    Traceable snapshots.

    Economic indicators come from the Bank of England and the Office for National Statistics. Every series carries its source, observation dates and units.

    The desk reads a stored snapshot, not a real-time feed. Series can have different publication schedules. Historical values may be revised after first publication.

    02 / THE FORECAST

    The future stays
    out of training.

    A chronological split separates training, validation and final testing. Lagged indicators estimate the next inflation observation, with simple baselines as a reference.

    Prediction intervals express empirical uncertainty. They are not a promise of coverage in a future regime. Revised historical data and assumed reporting delays limit any live-trading interpretation.

    03 / THE ANSWER

    Grounded in documents.
    Clear about limits.

    Questions retrieve relevant passages from public Bank of England, FCA and HM Treasury material. Supporting sources and excerpts remain visible so you can verify the answer.

    The answer identifies its mode: extractive evidence uses selected document text; optional language-model generation synthesises retrieved passages. Low-support or out-of-scope questions receive an abstention.

    Retrieval scores measure support within this corpus. They are not probabilities that an answer is correct.
    04 / THE PRODUCT

    Built to be inspected.
    Designed for research.

    A FastAPI service exposes indicators, forecasts, evaluation results and evidence retrieval. Stored data uses Parquet and DuckDB. Automated tests and container configuration support reproducible runs.

    This is an independent portfolio research project by Jayashruthi. It is not affiliated with the Bank of England, ONS or FCA.
